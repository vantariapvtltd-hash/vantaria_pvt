The Group

Vantaria is a privately held enterprise group headquartered in New Delhi. We make, source and sell fine confectionery and premium personal care, and we build the supply chains and storefronts that carry them to market.

Quality without compromise

Every product is held to export-grade standards, from formulation and production to the final seal on the pack.

Partnerships that compound

We grow alongside distributors, franchise partners and joint-venture partners who share a long view.

Integrated by design

Manufacturing, trade and commerce sit under one roof, so quality never leaves our hands.
